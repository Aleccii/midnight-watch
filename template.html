    Covers hourly · appearances dailyImpulse's catalog, Midtown's search and purchase control, and the skottieyoung.com storefront are re-read every hour; his Appearances page, the Planet Comicon guest page, FanCons and the Baltimore guest list daily. Alerts fire only on a new listing, a restock, a sold-out, a price or date change, or an edited appearance page.
    Active

    Scope: every Impulse and Midtown listing whose title credits Skottie Young on the cover, and the comics, artist exclusives, CGC Signature Series slabs and signed warehouse-sale books on his own store. Books he wrote with other cover artists, original art, prints, stickers and auctions are out of scope. Impulse's catalog API returns at most 100 pages per collection, so a back issue that sits only in the store's unnamed catalog can be missed; anything on preorder or in a publisher collection is covered.

    Audit trail

    Primary sources

    Method: listings are checked against the retailer's US storefront purchase control every 30 minutes (Shopify product JSON plus the visible add-to-cart state), never Shop.app metadata, foreign-currency pages, cached snippets, or aggregators. "Available" means an enabled US purchase path was seen at the last check; it does not guarantee checkout. Stock and allocations change without notice. Distinct variants and store-specific editions are never merged; suspected duplicates are flagged, not deleted. Informational only — not affiliated with Marvel, DC, any retailer, or Phillip Kennedy Johnson.
